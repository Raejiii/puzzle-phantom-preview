# Changelog

All notable changes to The Puzzle Phantom of Willowbrook are recorded here.

## Unreleased

### Added
- New game: a Phaser + ekshell port of the Godot detective story *The Puzzle Phantom of Willowbrook* from keytabdev/shounak-games. Follow the Phantom's notes across Willowbrook to win back the Golden Owl Trophy.
- 7 levels, one per puzzle in the original: three word scrambles (LEARN, MIDNIGHT, LIBRARY), a matter quiz, a compass-rose map path, a bakery maths quiz and the planet-order door.
- The original story word for word, with its voice-over, scene art and typewriter sound: narration and dialogue with the speakers on screen, the Phantom's notes, location titles, the unmasking at the Clock Tower and the epilogue. Tap to read, or skip it; the timer pauses during the story.
- The original music for each puzzle, its right and wrong sounds, and the spoken quiz questions and answers.
- The original's hint cards behind a Hint button, and its how-to clip for each puzzle behind How to (all five joined for the shell's How to play).
- The original's note pictures for the Phantom's notes, the planet door laid out as in the original, and the characters' blink frames.
- Auto-advance switch (remembered on the device) and a button to replay the current line.
- The race to midnight: a clock at the start of each level counting down to midnight. A case board that pins each level's clue, with a detective badge (gold star) for levels solved without hints.
- Separate layouts for landscape screens and phones held upright.
- Runtime art in `public/assets/` (about 935 KB, WebP), audio in `public/assets/audio/` (about 5 MB, MP3), how-to videos in `public/assets/video/` (about 0.8 MB), and the Special Elite and Fredoka fonts.
- Tests that every level can be played, that the puzzles match the original's answers, that every story line is voiced, that notes, videos, clock times and clues are all in place, and that images stay within 1 MB and audio and video within 7 MB.
